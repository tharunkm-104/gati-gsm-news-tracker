    Reading the numbers. Net developments balance = share of positive items minus share of negative items, in percentage points (pp). Developments are tagged against GATI's thesis — India's workforce as a solution to developed-market shortages — so a stricter US visa rule is negative even when the reporting is neutral. Figures count every item logged, so they can differ slightly from the dashboard if repeated stories are present (use “Hide repeated headlines” to strip them).
