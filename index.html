What "developments" mean here: each news item is tagged relative to GATI's core thesis — India's labour force as a solution to developed-market demographic shortages — not the tone of the article in general. A stricter US H-1B rule is "negative" even if it's neutral reporting; a new bilateral mobility pact is "positive."
Source: GATI Daily Mobility & Migration Briefing, produced by a scheduled Claude Cowork task that searches, ranks India-specific > destination-country > competitor-country > demographic news, tags each item 🔴🟡🟢, and posts to Slack.
